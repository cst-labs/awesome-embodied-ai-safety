# Embodied AI Safety Risk Topics and Reading Map

This page organizes embodied AI safety research into broad, mostly MECE risk categories. It is meant as an orientation aid for readers who want to connect technical risks, mitigation methods and representative papers.

The categories are not perfectly independent because embodied AI systems are stacks: data, models, perception, planning, control, deployment and governance interact. The table assigns each issue to the category where the risk primarily originates.

| Category | What It Covers | Key / Example Risks | Mitigation Methods | Representative Works |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capability and model risk | Core behavior of VLA models, robot foundation models, embodied LLM/VLM agents and world models | Unsafe action generation, poor grounding, hallucinated affordances, overgeneralization, literal instruction following, weak refusal behavior | Safety fine-tuning, constrained action generation, robot constitutions, semantic safety filters, task precondition checks, model cards | [Vision-Language-Action Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23775); [SafeVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03480); [Gemini Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20020); [Cosmos 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02800); [Embodied-R1.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11324) |
| Data and training risk | Risks introduced through pretraining, robot demonstrations, imitation data, instruction tuning, RL, fine-tuning and checkpoints | Poisoned demonstrations, backdoors, hidden triggers, unsafe preference data, biased training distributions, compromised model supply chain | Dataset provenance, trusted data pipelines, data filtering, backdoor scanning, trigger tests, secure checkpoint handling, adversarial validation sets | [Vision-Language-Action Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23775); [Instructions as Backdoors](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.171/); [SHINE](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/yuan24c.html); [Open X-Embodiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08864) |
| Perception and world-model risk | How the system interprets scenes, objects, people, state, dynamics and future outcomes from multimodal inputs | Adversarial patches, sensor spoofing, occlusion, lighting failures, hallucinated scene state, semantic grounding errors, cross-modal conflicts, incorrect physical prediction | Sensor fusion validation, cross-modal consistency checks, adversarial training, uncertainty estimation, physical-world stress testing, semantic grounding benchmarks, world-model evaluation | [Adversarial Attacks on Multimodal LLMs](https://research.google/pubs/adversarial-attacks-on-multimodal-large-language-models-a-comprehensive-survey/); [Physical-world AV attack survey](https://researchportal.ip-paris.fr/en/publications/adversarial-attacks-on-autonomous-driving-systems-in-the-physical/); [Cosmos 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02800); [3D-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09631); [RoboSemanticBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02277) |
| Planning and action risk | Translation of goals, language, perception and model outputs into physical plans, trajectories, tool use and actuator commands | Collisions, excessive force, unstable grasping, unsafe navigation, bad sequencing, wrong object manipulation, unsafe tool use, unsafe long-horizon manipulation | Control barrier functions, safe motion planning, action shielding, affordance checks, constrained RL, trajectory validation, low-level safety controllers | [SafeVLA-Bench](https://safevla.org/); [RoboGuard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07885); [Learning Control Barrier Functions and their Application in RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16879); [Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05660); [Embedding ISO 10218 Safety Compliance in Robots via Control Barrier Functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13203); [PACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08414) |

| Runtime and deployment risk | Failures that appear during real-world operation after lab testing or simulation | Sim-to-real failure, distribution shift, model drift, out-of-competence states, late failure detection, poor fallback behavior, failed recovery | Runtime monitors, failure detectors, anomaly detection, emergency stop, fallback controllers, staged deployment, post-deployment monitoring, recovery policies | [SAFE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09937); [ActProbe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08508); [Hide-and-Seek in Trajectories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30834); [FailSafe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01642); [RoboArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18123) |
| Adversarial misuse and security risk | Intentional attempts to manipulate, compromise or misuse embodied AI systems | Jailbreaks, prompt injection, malicious instructions, voice attacks, cyber-physical compromise, unsafe API/tool access | Embodied red teaming, refusal tuning, input filtering, authentication, least privilege, secure control planes, penetration testing, threat modeling | [BadRobot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20242); [Jailbreaking LLM-Controlled Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13691); [Embodied Red Teaming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18676); [MITRE ATLAS](https://atlas.mitre.org/); [FORESIGHT](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/ndss-paper/auto-draft-604/) |

## Recent Update Notes

The latest June 2026 additions mostly strengthen five categories:

- Capability and model risk: [Embodied-R1.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11324) adds a frontier open embodied foundation model release to track alongside Cosmos, Gemini Robotics, OpenVLA and pi0.
- Perception and world-model risk: [RoboSemanticBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02277) highlights semantic grounding failures in VLA action prediction.
- Planning and action risk: [Embedding ISO 10218 Safety Compliance in Robots via Control Barrier Functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13203) and [PACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08414) add concrete safety-control and diffusion-policy alignment work.
- Evaluation and assurance gaps: [Benchmarking VLA Models on SO-101](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08881), [RoboArena](https://robo-arena.github.io/) and [EmbodiedEvalKit](https://github.com/pickxiguapi/EmbodiedEvalKit) strengthen real-world and benchmark-infrastructure coverage.
- Runtime and deployment risk: [ActProbe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08508) and [SAFE](https://github.com/vla-safe/SAFE) strengthen early failure detection and runtime safety coverage.
